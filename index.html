About Us

Our Three Pillars of Action

1. Invest in Community Solutions

We invest directly in neighbor-led solutions, honoring community health champions and providing small grants that turn local ideas into real-world public health initiatives.

2. Champion Health & Social Equity

We mobilize local networks to advocate for health in all policies, tackling root causes of disparities and strengthening climate resilience across every Brookline neighborhood.

3. Educate and Unite Neighbors

We bridge the gap between technical science and daily life through open public forums and our signature "Inside Brookline Health" podcast, giving residents actionable information.

Invest in Community Solutions

Where you live, learn, work, and play directly shapes your well-being. Health is not determined in a doctor's office alone; it is driven by everyday social conditions.

Direct Aid & Health Initiatives

● Food Security & Holiday Relief

Annual donation campaigns like Brookline Bounty Birds Drive supply Thanksgiving/winter meals. We support Farm to Families, emergency grocery cards, and the Walk for Hunger.

● Essential Hygiene Access

"Change is Good" drives collect diapers and senior incontinence supplies, relieving financial burdens not covered by federal programs like SNAP.

● Neighborhood Safety & Mini-Grants

Providing CPR equipment, parenting programs, housing authority challenges, and seed micro-funding for creative wellness solutions by residents.

Champion Health & Social Equity

True health equity means ensuring that every resident has fair and just opportunities to achieve their optimal health, regardless of race, background, income, or ZIP code.

When town policies shape our housing, air quality, transit, and public spaces, those decisions directly impact well-being. Through civic leadership and coalition building, we embed public health priorities into decision-making.

Health in All Policies

By advocating for public health priorities across town planning, environmental design, and social services, we ensure every resident has the opportunity to thrive in a safe and supportive environment.

Educate and Unite Neighbors

We fill the gap between science and daily life through open forums and our signature podcast, giving residents actionable information to protect their families.

  • Community Health Forums The Hidden Cost of Plastics to Human Health: Partnering with Mothers Out Front, we brought residents and environmental health advocates together to explore how microplastics impact human health and examine concrete steps our town can take to reduce exposure. Addressing Vaccine Hesitancy as a Global & Local Challenge: Partnering with Rotary, we hosted a community dialogue examining the science of immunizations, countering skepticism with transparent data, and building trust in lifesaving vaccines. A Rotating Educational Agenda: Topics include youth mental health, environmental safety, emergency preparedness, and family wellness.
  • Uniting Neighbors Through Civic Engagement Education works hand-in-hand with community action. Our educational programs bring together diverse perspectives, fostering empathy, mutual support, and collaboration across neighborhoods, school networks, and local organizations to uplift town-wide health.

Podcast

Inside Brookline Health

As part of our multimedia outreach, we produce "Inside Brookline Health," a local podcast created to reach residents in their homes, on their commutes, or on daily walks. Each episode translates complex research into plain language, delivering fact-based insights on local health services, wellness strategies, and collective community resilience.
